Privacy Policy

NeuroOncall for iOS · Last updated October 10, 2026

NeuroOncall is a neurology reference app for clinicians. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

Information stored on your device

  • The first name you enter on the welcome screen, used only to personalize the greeting.
  • Your bookmarks and recently viewed pages.

This information is stored locally on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party, and it is removed when you delete the app.

No accounts, tracking or analytics

The app has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs, and it does not access your location, contacts, camera, photos or health data.

Patient information

Do not enter patient-identifying information into the app. The app has no feature for storing patient data.

Children

The app is intended for healthcare professionals and trainees and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
